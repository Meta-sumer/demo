# Early close (Sell now) back-test and calibration

Generated by `server-go/cmd/calibrate -early-close` on 2026-10-23T05:25:02Z. Machine-readable copy: `early-close-backtest.json`.
The formula, the method and the conclusions are in `early-close.md`; this file holds every table.

- Data: Binance BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT 1-second klines 2026-10-02 00:00 to 2026-10-16 00:00 (the live volatility estimator) and 1-minute
  klines 2024-10-16 00:00 to 2026-10-16 00:00 (proxy volatility: last 60 one-minute returns).
- Valuation: `binary.QuoteEarlyClose` with `binary.EarlyCloseCorrection`, fee 5.0%, P(win) clamped to [0.01, 0.99],
  cut-off 10 s, stake 100000 minor units, payout = the engine's calm payout for the asset and expiry.
- Periods: FIT = entries in the first 60% of each window (regime cuts, the correction), TEST = the rest.
- Edge = payoff if held - value paid, percent of stake (positive: the platform pays less than the position is worth).
- Brier and log loss are for "the position wins" (a draw is not a win); a no-information P = 0.5 scores 0.25 and 0.6931.
- "At the strike": the current price within one tick of the entry (klines carry the last trade, which bounces
  between bid and ask, so a one-tick difference is usually no move of the mid the live engine prices on). These
  valuations form their own bin and are left out of the probability bins and of the fit.
