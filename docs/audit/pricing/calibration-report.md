## 2. Baseline volatility (specification section 15)

`baseline_vol` is the realised per-second volatility over the recent 1-second window: sqrt(mean of squared
1-second log returns). The engine's EWMA starts there and `vol_ratio = sigma / baseline_vol` drives `vol_adj`.

configured defaults (`models.DefaultPricingSymbolRules`) are the values above. Per-second volatility measured
from 1-minute returns over two years is higher (different period, and returns are not independent at the
sub-minute scale): re-run this calibration regularly and change `baselineVol` through the versioned config.

## 3. Back-test of the payout formula (live estimator, 1-second data)

Every sampled second of the 1-second window is priced exactly as live: the EWMA (lambda 0.97, one observation a
second) at that second, the formula with the default parameters and the seeded payout row as base, an empty book
and a one-tick spread. Its real outcome is then booked with draws refunded. Regimes are terciles of vol_ratio over
the offered samples. E = (1 - p_win - p_draw) - p_win x mean payout, per unit stake, for a customer always buying
UP and always buying DOWN; "calm E" is E at the highest payout the formula offers (vol_ratio <= 1), which bounds
every offer from below because E falls as the payout rises. Half-width: 95% at the effective sample size
(samples x min(1, stride / expiry)).

fall to the minimum; the lowest regime E barely moves, because vol_adj only ever lowers the payout.

## 5. The guard model (F-02)

The payout follows the formula; a calibrated win/draw-probability model is kept as a safety guard: an offer
whose payout would leave less than the configured edge (1% of stake) at the model's probabilities is blocked.
Method: features from the window before entry (1-second grid for expiries under 5 minutes, 1-minute grid
otherwise; volatility, momentum z-score, local draw rate); P(UP | no draw) logistic in momentum (drift and
momentum kept only if they beat simpler forms on validation); P(draw) logistic in ln(sigma) and the local
draw rate. Three periods in time order: 60% train (parameters), 20% holdout (uncertainty add-ons u and dq per
volatility tercile), 20% test (every metric below). Brier and log loss are for "UP wins" against the no-skill
baseline (1 - q) / 2.

## 7. Limits

- Two weeks of 1-second data and two years of 1-minute data, ending 2026-10-16 00:00 UTC. A crash, an exchange outage or a
  regime not in these windows is not represented; the block ratio and the guard refuse what is far outside them.
- Klines carry no order book: the back-test assumes a one-tick spread (the live engine uses the real book).
- The back-test prices against an empty book: exposure_adj only lowers payouts further, so E only rises.
- hedge_cost is the configured placeholder (3.5 points for crypto); the Binance venue cost is far lower
  (2 x (taker fee + half spread + slippage), about 0.2 to 0.3 points at the default hedge ratios). Reducing it is
  a business decision; this report shows the margin it leaves.
- The guard's probabilities and add-ons are statistical estimates on the periods above, not guarantees.
